Cancellations & Refunds Policy

Effective Date: December 27, 2025

Digital Content

All digital content purchases (e-books, digital editions, online courses, and media subscriptions) are non-refundable. Due to the nature of digital goods, once access is granted, refunds cannot be processed.

Physical Books

For physical book purchases, refunds may be available within 30 days of delivery, provided the item is returned in its original condition. Shipping costs for returns are the responsibility of the customer.

Cancellation Process

To request a cancellation or refund for eligible items, please contact us at admin@gmail.com with your order details. We will review your request and respond within 5-7 business days.

Refund Processing

Approved refunds for physical items will be processed within 10-14 business days after receipt and inspection of the returned item. Refunds will be issued to the original payment method.

Exceptions

Refunds may be considered on a case-by-case basis for technical issues, incorrect orders, or damaged physical items. Please provide detailed information when requesting an exception.

Contact Information

For any refund or cancellation inquiries, please email us at admin@gmail.com or call 12341234.
